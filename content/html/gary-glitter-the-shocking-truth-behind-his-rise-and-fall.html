This dissonance highlights the danger of separating art from the artist when the artist’s actions cause significant harm to others. ## The Legal Reckoning The turning point in Gary Glitter’s life came with a series of legal battles that stripped away his celebrity status and replaced it with the label of a convicted sex offender. In 2008, he was sentenced to nine years in prison for raping two teenage girls in Thailand. This conviction marked the beginning of the end for his career.
